# Diet App

Five Two Table is a browser-based 5:2 meal planner. Choose two nonconsecutive reduced-calorie days, enter your own targets, and log portions to compare calories, protein, carbs, fat, and fiber. The app can suggest meals for a day or a week.

Open index.html directly, or run `python -m http.server 8769 --bind 127.0.0.1` and visit <http://localhost:8769/>.

Nutrition values for sample meals are estimates. Your log stays in your browser. The schedule is based on the [Cleveland Clinic 5:2 guide](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/5-2-diet).
